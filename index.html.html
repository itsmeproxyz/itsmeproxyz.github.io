Biography

I'm Dimas, but online everyone knows me as proxyz. I'm based in Bekasi, and when I'm not at my desk I'm usually logged into some other world.

MMORPGs are where I feel most at home. I love the huge open maps, the long grinds, the guild runs that somehow go way past midnight, and that one gear upgrade that finally drops after a hundred tries. There's nothing quite like watching a character grow from a nobody into someone the server recognizes.

When I need a quicker hit of adrenaline, I switch to tactical shooters. One clean headshot or a 1v3 clutch can make the whole night. Patient in the grind, sharp in the firefight: that's the proxyz way.
